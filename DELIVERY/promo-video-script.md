## Capture requirements

- Record a clean 1920x1080 Game View at 60 fps.
- Use a fresh save and capture the scene without editor overlays or debug keys.
- Record each shot with 2 seconds of handles before and after the intended action.
- Capture separate game audio and narration tracks.
- Replace the closing call to action with a real store or community URL before release.
- Export H.264 MP4, 16-20 Mbps, AAC audio; review captions and all UI text at full size.
